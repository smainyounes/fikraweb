How we work

Four steps, from discovery to ongoing support.

  1. Step 01

    Discovery

    We start by understanding your business: how you’re organised, your teams, the way you work today and what costs you time or money.

    • In person, by phone or on WhatsApp
    • No technical document to prepare
  2. Step 02

    Demo

    We show you the software suited to your trade, with examples close to your business. For a custom project, we present the proposed solution and its scope.

    • In person or remotely
    • Your questions, your real cases
  3. Step 03

    Installation or development

    For ready-to-use software, we install it and set it up with your information. For a custom project, we develop it in stages you approve.

    • Your name, your logo, your settings
    • Custom: stages approved one by one
  4. Step 04

    Training & support

    We train your team to use the software, then stay available to answer your questions and support you over time.

    • Team training included
    • Local support on WhatsApp

For a custom project, development follows six detailed steps, from analysis to maintenance.
