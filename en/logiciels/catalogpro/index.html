FAQ

Your questions about CatalogPro.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can one customer see another customer’s prices?

No. Each price list has its own secret link, and you can make a link expire or switch it off at any time.

Can I hide prices on the public website?

Yes. Catalogue prices can be shown to everyone or hidden: your customers then only see the prices on their own list.

How do I send a price list?

You copy the link and send it on WhatsApp, or e-mail it from CatalogPro if e-mail sending is set up.

Is the website available in Arabic?

The public website is available in French and Arabic. The management area is in French.
