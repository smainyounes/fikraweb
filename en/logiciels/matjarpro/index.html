FAQ

Your questions about MatjarPro.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Does MatjarPro take a commission on my sales?

No. You pay for the installation and the monthly subscription, and no percentage is taken on your sales.

Which delivery companies are supported?

MatjarPro connects to Algerian delivery companies through a gateway: Yalidine, ZR Express, DHD, Maystro, Ecotrack platforms and others. You need an account with the company you choose.

Do my customers need to create an account?

No. They order straight from the product page, with their name, phone number, wilaya and commune.

How does MatjarPro help against fake orders?

You see each number’s history, you can limit the number of orders per number per day, and block a number after returned parcels.

Is the store in French?

The store and the management area are in Arabic, the language of most online shoppers in Algeria. The language can be adapted to your needs: just ask us.
