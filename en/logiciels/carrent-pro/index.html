What CarRent Pro changes

Everyday problems, solved.

  • Before

    You check whether a car is available by calling the agency or leafing through a notebook.

    With CarRent Pro

    Availability is calculated from bookings and maintenance, and a planner shows the next 14 days.

  • Before

    Contracts are filled in by hand when the keys are handed over.

    With CarRent Pro

    The contract is created at handover, with the mileage, and prints as a PDF.

  • Before

    Deposits and balances owed get lost between rentals.

    With CarRent Pro

    Every deposit is tracked (collected, refunded or retained) and every invoice shows its balance.

  • Before

    A customer without a valid licence, or already flagged, drives off with a car.

    With CarRent Pro

    On confirmation, the software checks the licence, the driver’s age and the list of blocked customers.

FAQ

Your questions about CarRent Pro.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Can my customers book directly online?

They can send a booking request from your website. The request arrives in CarRent Pro, and your team turns it into a booking, or declines it.

Is the management interface in Arabic?

The management area is in French. Your agency’s public website is available in French and Arabic.

Is my customers’ data protected?

ID and licence numbers are encrypted and masked on screen. Showing them requires a permission, and every viewing is recorded in the activity log.

Can I limit what my staff can see?

Yes. Each staff member has their own permissions, and some actions (such as cancelling an invoice) are reserved for administrators.

Are late returns charged?

When the vehicle comes back, the software calculates late days, missing fuel and the damage entered. These charges can then be invoiced.
