Who it’s for

For agencies that handle groups, departures and a lot of paperwork.

  • 01

    Umrah and Hajj agencies

    Pilgrims, groups, guides, hotels and rooms, season after season.

  • 02

    Organised tour agencies

    Tours and excursions in Algeria: departures, coaches, pick-up points.

  • 03

    International travel agencies

    Packages, plane tickets, hotels, insurance and visa applications.

What SafarDesk changes

Everyday problems, solved.

  • Before

    Pilgrim and room lists are redone by hand in Excel for every departure.

    With SafarDesk

    Groups, rooms and lists are kept in SafarDesk, and documents print directly.

  • Before

    Nobody knows which family has paid what, or what is still to be collected.

    With SafarDesk

    Every payment is assigned to a booking, and each customer’s statement shows their balance.

  • Before

    Visa files go back and forth between offices with no tracking.

    With SafarDesk

    Each file has its list of documents to provide and its status.

  • Before

    No way of knowing whether a departure was profitable.

    With SafarDesk

    Expenses and income are linked to the departure, with a profitability report.

FAQ

Your questions about SafarDesk.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Is SafarDesk suitable for a small agency?

Yes. You only use the modules you need. An agency that only does Umrah doesn’t have to use tours or ticketing.

Can I manage several branches?

Yes. Branches are managed in the same software, with users, roles and permissions for each.

Which documents can I print?

Including quotes, travel contracts, invoices, payment receipts, passenger lists, rooming lists, departure manifests, supplier purchase orders and customer statements.

My team mostly speaks Arabic. Is that a problem?

No. SafarDesk is available in French and Arabic, and so is its built-in user guide.

Can I tell whether a departure was profitable?

Yes. A departure’s income and expenses are linked to it, and the reports calculate its profitability.
