FAQ

Your questions about GaragePro.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need to install anything on my computers?

No. GaragePro is an online application: you use it in a web browser, on a computer, tablet or phone. An internet connection is required.

Can my customers book appointments online?

They can send an appointment request from the garage website by picking an available slot. You then confirm it in GaragePro.

Can I keep track of what customers owe me?

Yes. Payments are recorded against invoices, and a list shows every customer who still has an amount to pay.

Does the software handle parts sold over the counter?

Yes. Counter sales take parts out of stock, just like the parts used in repairs.

Is the website in Arabic?

The software is available in French and Arabic. Your website’s text can be edited from the app.
