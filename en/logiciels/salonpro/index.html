FAQ

Your questions about SalonPro.

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Does SalonPro work without internet?

Yes. SalonPro is installed directly on your Windows PC or Android tablet. It works fully offline, and your data stays on the device.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. We start with a demo. Once installed, the app runs for 15 days before it needs activating, and your data is kept after activation.

Is there a tablet version?

Yes. SalonPro comes as a Windows app for computers and an Android app for tablets, handy as a touch-screen till at the counter. Both work offline.

What does “licence per device” mean?

The licence is activated for one specific device: a PC or a tablet. If you change devices, a new activation is needed: get in touch with us.

Can my staff see the takings?

Only if you decide so. Each user has a role, and you choose each role’s permissions, including access to financial figures.

Can I manage my product stock or salaries?

No. SalonPro focuses on appointments, the till, clients and expenses. It doesn’t handle stock, payroll or multiple salons. For a specific need, ask us about custom development.

Can clients book online?

No. Appointments are entered by the salon. SalonPro doesn’t send SMS reminders either.
