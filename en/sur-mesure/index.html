Custom development

A specific need? We develop the right solution.

Every business has its own processes, teams and constraints. Rather than you adapting to software, we design the software around the way you work.

Tell us about my project Chat on WhatsApp

Custom software is for you if…

  • Your way of working doesn’t fit any standard software.
  • Several departments each work in their own notebooks or Excel files.
  • You want a tool under your own name that grows with your business.
  • You need to connect tools that don’t talk to each other.

One of our ready-to-use software products can also serve as a starting point, then be adapted.

What we build

Management software, not brochure websites.

We build the systems that keep a business running day to day.

  • ERP and management systems

    One tool connecting purchasing, stock, production, sales and finance.

  • Internal applications

    The tools your teams need every day, instead of notebooks and Excel files.

  • Stock and warehouses

    Goods in and out, several warehouses, stock counts and order picking.

  • Production management

    Bills of materials, production orders, material consumption.

  • Sales and finance

    Quotes, proformas, delivery notes, receipts, cash desk and cash management.

  • HR and attendance

    Staff, teams, attendance, leave, payroll and salary advances.

  • Dashboards and reports

    Your business’s key figures, at the right time and for the right people.

  • Platforms and web applications

    Customer portals, partner areas, custom online platforms.

  • Integration and automation

    Less repeated data entry: your tools exchange data automatically.

Our method

A project run step by step, with you.

  1. 01

    First conversation

    You tell us about your business and what’s holding you back today. No specification document needed.

  2. 02

    Analysis and scope

    We study your processes with you and define exactly what the software must do.

  3. 03

    Proposal and quote

    You receive a clear proposal: features, stages, timing and price.

  4. 04

    Development in stages

    The software is built in stages. You approve each one before moving on to the next.

  5. 05

    Testing and go-live

    The software is tested with your real cases, then installed and put into service.

  6. 06

    Training and maintenance

    Your teams are trained, and we provide follow-up and maintenance.

Case study · Custom project

ERP for a furniture manufacturer

Custom project · not for sale

The need

Connect every step of the business in one system: buying raw materials, stock, manufacturing, sales, delivery, the cash desk and staff management.

What we built

An ERP designed around the workshop’s processes, where every department works on the same data, with access limited by each person’s role.

What it shows

Our ability to analyse a whole organisation and deliver a complex management system, module by module.

The modules in detail

Production

  • Items and categories
  • Bills of materials: materials and quantities per product
  • Printable production orders
  • Material consumption and finished goods

Stock

  • Several warehouses
  • Stock movements
  • Stock count, with file import
  • Stock per item

Purchasing and suppliers

  • Purchase orders
  • Goods received
  • Orders awaiting receipt
  • Supplier payments

Sales and deliveries

  • Proformas
  • Delivery notes
  • Orders to prepare
  • Stock dispatches and deliveries

Cash desk and cash management

  • Receipts and payment slips
  • Cash desk queue
  • Cash accounts
  • Cash desk documents

Attendance

  • Time-clock data import
  • Daily and monthly attendance

Staff and payroll

  • Staff records and teams
  • Payroll calculation
  • Salary advances
  • Pay breakdown per employee

Oversight and security

  • Dashboard and reports

FAQ

Custom development, in practice.

How much does custom software cost?

There is no fixed price: each project is quoted according to its scope. After analysing your need, you receive a detailed proposal with the stages, timing and price.

Do I need a specification document?

No. Explain your business and your difficulties in your own words. We define together what the software must do.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the project. Timing is set out in the proposal, and development moves forward in stages you approve.

Can I start with just one part?

Yes. We can start with the most useful module, then add the others step by step.

What happens after go-live?

Your teams are trained, and we provide follow-up and maintenance for the software.

Can one of your existing products be adapted?

Yes, it’s often the quickest option: we start from one of our products and adapt it to your needs. Tell us about your project.
