FAQ

Pricing and subscription.

All questions

How does the subscription work?

For the online software (GaragePro, CarRent Pro, SafarDesk, MatjarPro and CatalogPro), you pay an installation fee, then a monthly subscription that is required to run the software. SalonPro has no subscription: it’s a lifetime licence for one device, with optional support of about 6,000 DZD a year.

How does installation work?

After the demo, we install the software: on your device for SalonPro, online for the others. We then set it up with your information (name, logo, prices, staff, settings), and train your team.

Is training included?

Yes. Training for your team is part of the installation. After that, we stay available on WhatsApp for your questions.
